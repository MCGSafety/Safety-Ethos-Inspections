Step 1 · Awareness

Every employee is empowered to recognize hazards

Check Yes if no hazards are observed in the category, No if any at-risk behavior is present. If No, tally how many observations and describe them.

Step 2 · Action

We own safety together by taking action

For each at-risk item, indicate if you're qualified to take action. Qualified means feeling informed and comfortable enough to act immediately. If not, name who's accountable.

Step 3 · Accountability

If it's not safe, stop work

Stop Work Authority (SWA) is the right and responsibility to stop any task posing an immediate safety, health, or environmental risk. Log every at-risk behavior observed.
